Unforgettable Experiences 🎯

🌅 Rooftop Chill Vibes

Relax on our stunning rooftop overlooking the Atlantic Ocean in Taghazout. Perfect sunsets, good vibes, and amazing company. Watch the world slow down as you sip local mint tea and share stories with fellow surfers.

Location: Taghazout Rooftop Terrace

🏄‍♂️ Professional Surf Lessons

Learn from certified instructors right on the beaches of Taghazout, Imsouan, and Tamraghte. Perfect waves for all levels, from beginners to advanced surfers. Small groups ensure personalized attention and rapid progress.

Location: Tamraghte & Imsouan Beaches

🥐 Fresh Breakfast Experience

Start your day with authentic Moroccan and international breakfast. Fresh pastries, local bread, homemade jams, and energizing smoothies. Fueled and ready for a perfect day of surfing and exploration.

Location: OCEVIA House, Taghazout

🍽️ Sunset Dinner by the Ocean

Enjoy culinary excellence with fresh seafood and traditional Moroccan dishes. Every dinner is a celebration with views of the Atlantic and the sound of waves. Share stories and connect with your fellow surfers under the stars.

Location: Oceanfront Terrace, Imsouan

🚴‍♂️ Adventure & Exploration

Discover hidden gems around Taghazout, Tamraghte, and Imsouan. Hike coastal cliffs, explore Berber villages, visit local markets, and immerse yourself in authentic Moroccan culture. Adventure awaits every corner.

Location: Taghazout Region
